About

A NYC-based Artist Development Company

Anemoia was launched in 2023 by respected Atlantic Records A&R Executive Ian Swift Hunter. Founded on the principles of shared ownership, aligned incentives, and sustainable creative infrastructure, the New York City-based label specializes in the early development of artists whose innovative music and singular visions place them at the forefront of their emerging scenes.

Through unconventional deal structures and bespoke support services, Anemoia helps build and scale multiple revenue streams unique to each artist brand.
